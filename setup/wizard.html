Before we start

The Co-Pilot is not a signals app. It does not tell you what to trade. It watches your own rules and refuses entries that break them.

That means it needs two numbers from you for every trade you take: where you are wrong (your stop) and how much you are risking (your size). The next steps collect those.

Have your prop firm dashboard open. You will be asked for your account's daily loss limit, drawdown threshold and profit target. Guessing these is how accounts die.
