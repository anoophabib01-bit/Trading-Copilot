Journals tell you on Sunday what you did wrong on Tuesday. That is too late. Co-Pilot reads your live chart and your rulebook, and it answers one question while your finger is on the button: is this trade allowed?

⏳

Post-loss cooldown

After a loss the app locks entries for a set period. Revenge trading is not talked out of you, it is physically blocked.

⛔

News blackout

Red-folder events force a NO-GO. The app will not let you hold through a release you said you would not hold through.

📏

Oversize guard

Position size is checked against your cap, not your mood. Exceed it and the entry is refused, with the number that broke it shown.

✅

Plan-adherence grading

A losing trade that followed every rule is a good trade. A winner that broke them is a bad one. You get scored on the rules, not the P&L.

"Grade the session on plan-adherence, not P&L. Broke the cap or took setups outside the plan and made money anyway = loss, full stop, regardless of what the account shows." — the rule the whole app is built around

Questions

Does this tell me what to trade?

No, and deliberately so. There are a thousand signal services. Adding another one would make your problem worse, not better. Co-Pilot only ever answers whether the trade you have already chosen is allowed by the rules you set.

Will it show me a track record or profit curve?

No. Be careful with anyone in this industry who leads with one. You will see your own discipline score over time — how often you followed your own rules — which is the only number here we can honestly verify.

What do I need to run it?

Windows, TradingView Desktop, and about ten minutes for setup. The setup wizard checks all of it before you pay anything and tells you exactly what is missing.

Does it place trades?

It can, but only if you deliberately switch that on, and it is off by default. The core product never touches your orders — it only refuses to approve entries.

What if I already have a journal?

Keep it. Co-Pilot is not a journal and does not try to be. Use both: they answer different questions, at different times, about the same day.

What happens after 12 months on Local?

Your licence keeps working forever. You stop receiving updates. If you want them continued, a renewal is available at half the original price — that pays for the ongoing work of keeping up with TradingView, which does change.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, within 14 days, no questions. If it does not fit how you trade, we would rather have the feedback than your money.
