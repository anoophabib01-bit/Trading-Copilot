# The five-trader test

The research is done. Nine competitor pages, verified revenue where it exists, a feature map, a
setup wizard, a free tier and a pricing page. None of it tells you whether a real trader who is
not you can install this and get value from it. Five people do.

**Do not ask these five people to pay.** The goal is information, not revenue. Free access in
exchange for honesty.

---

## Who to approach

Pick people from where you already are. Do not cold-DM strangers first.

| Where | Why |
|---|---|
| Your own trading Discord / Telegram groups | They know you, so they will actually try it |
| r/FuturesTrading, r/Daytrading, r/PropFirmTrading | Read the rules first; many ban self-promotion |
| Prop firm communities (Apex, Topstep Discords) | Exactly your buyer |
| Anyone you know who has blown an evaluation | They have the pain, which is the point |

**The filter that matters:** they must have blown at least one evaluation, and they must be able to
say why. Someone who has never lost control of their size is not your user and will give you
useless feedback.

---

## The message

Keep it short. Do not lead with the product. Lead with the question.

### First contact (DM or post)

> Hey [name] — I trade MNQ on an Apex eval and I kept breaking my own rules at the worst
> moments, so I built a tool that watches my rules during the session and refuses entries that
> break them. It is not a signals thing, it does not tell you what to trade.
>
> I am looking for 5 traders to try it free, no strings, and tell me honestly whether it is
> useful or annoying. Can I send you the setup?
>
> One question first, because it decides if this is even for you: **have you ever taken a trade
> you knew broke your own rules? What happened?**

That last question is the whole qualification. If they say "no" or "I'm disciplined", move on.

### When they say yes

> Great. Two things:
>
> 1. It needs Windows + TradingView Desktop. Setup is a wizard — it checks your machine and
>    writes your rules. I need to know **how long it takes you and where you get stuck**, so
>    please do not let me help you through it.
> 2. Once you're in, use it for a week on your normal trading. I want to know when it is wrong,
>    when it is annoying, and whether you ignored it.

Delivery: send them the folder + `SETUP.bat`. If they cannot get past setup, **that is the most
valuable result you can get** — it is the blocker that kills the product, and you want to learn
it from five people, not five hundred.

---

## What to ask after a week

Ask the problem questions, not the solution questions. Never ask "do you like it".

| Ask | What you are learning |
|---|---|
| Walk me through your last session with it open. | Whether they actually used it |
| When did it tell you NO-GO? What did you do? | Whether the core mechanic lands |
| Did you override it? What happened next? | The upgrade moment, or proof there isn't one |
| What did you ignore or find annoying? | Feature kills |
| What did it fail to catch? | Whether your rules model is complete |
| Did it change any decision? | The only real measure of value |
| What would you pay, if anything? | Pricing, from a user instead of a guess |
| If I turned it off tomorrow, would you notice? | The honest keep-or-kill question |

---

## What to measure

| Metric | Target | If it fails |
|---|---|---|
| Got through setup unaided | 4 of 5 | The CDP dependency is the product problem. Fix that before anything else. |
| Time to first useful session | under 20 min | Wizard needs work |
| Used it on 2+ sessions | 3 of 5 | It is not sticky yet |
| Saw a real NO-GO | 3 of 5 | Your rules model does not match how they trade |
| Ignored a NO-GO | any | This is GOOD. That is the upgrade moment. |
| Would pay $149 once | 2 of 5 | Reprice, or the market is wrong |

### The one result that changes everything

If **fewer than 3 of 5 can get through setup**, stop building features. The TradingView Desktop
and port 9222 dependency is what stands between you and every customer, and no amount of scoring
or polish fixes it. That is the signal to build the Cloud tier next.

If **4 or 5 get through and 2 would pay**, you have a product. Then it is a marketing problem.

---

## What not to do

- Do not send it to people who will be nice to you. Nice feedback is worse than none.
- Do not help them past setup. The friction is the finding.
- Do not add features during the test week. You will not be able to tell what changed.
- Do not ask "would you buy this". Ask what they would pay, and then stay quiet.
- Do not launch publicly on the strength of five friendly responses. Five is a signal, not proof.

---

## After the five

| Outcome | Next move |
|---|---|
| 3+ through setup, 2+ would pay | Charge. Gumroad, Founding 50 at $99, then $149. |
| Setup blocks 2+ people | Build Cloud. Drop the local install requirement. |
| Nobody overrides a NO-GO | The enforcement is not biting. Re-examine whether it is firing at the right moments. |
| They find it annoying, not useful | Wrong problem, or wrong user. Go back to the questions. |
| All five say they are disciplined | You picked the wrong five, or the market is smaller than it looks. |
