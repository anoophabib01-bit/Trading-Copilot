# 90-Day Apprenticeship — Lucid Prop Mastery via the Co-Pilot
*(2026-07-17. One framework. No new ones for 90 days — consuming another guru mid-plan is a rule break, logged like any other.)*

## The learning-science engine (mapped to app features you already have)

| Technique | What it means here | App mechanism |
|---|---|---|
| **Active recall** | Before each session, write your rules from memory BEFORE opening the checklist, then check yourself against it | Checklist tab = the answer key. Score yourself /10 in the plan notes |
| **Spaced repetition** | Rules resurface at expanding intervals: daily (loop focus), weekly (Monday digest), monthly (full review) | Loop focus banner · scheduled 21:30 reconcile · Insights history |
| **Feynman technique** | After every trade day: explain each trade in ONE sentence a 12-year-old would understand. Can't explain it simply = it wasn't a setup, it was an impulse | Playbook tagging — a trade you can't tag A/B/C gets tagged as a mistake |
| **Interleaving** | Don't drill one pattern repeatedly; mix regimes — trending day, chop day, news day — in replay so recognition becomes discrimination | TradingView replay tools (replay_start / replay_step / replay_trade) already wired into your MCP |

## Stage 1 — Days 1–21: "One Clean Rep" (the 5-green-day challenge, extended)
**Goal: 15 sessions where process grade ≥ B. P&L explicitly does not count.**
- Daily: active-recall rules test (60 sec) → checklist → max 2-3 shots, 19:00–20:00 IST, 6 micros flat, one direction → CSV upload → read score + focus → one-sentence Feynman note per trade (2 min, in the day's plan notes).
- Replay drill (3×/week, NOT during session hours): pick a random past NY open in TradingView replay, trade your playbook with replay_trade, 2 shots max. This is where volume-of-reps lives — sim reps are free, live reps cost eval fees.
- Weekly (Saturday, 30 min): Insights review — score trend, avgW:avgL ratio, red-day causes. Write ONE sentence: "next week I will fix ___." One.
- **Stage gate:** 5-green-day streak achieved + zero direction-flip days in the final week. Pass this and the eval math ($600/day pace) is already handling itself.

## Stage 2 — Days 22–50: "Size the Winner, Not the Ego"
**Goal: avgW:avgL ≥ 1.5x. This is the profitability switch.**
- Now the MAE/MFE loop matters most: after each session, compute it. Target capture% climbing week over week (35% → 50%+). Method: JadeCap's risk-free add — stop to break-even on entry #1, add only after confirmation, never past 6 total.
- Hold drill: on every winner, hold until either the next level or a 4H/1H candle-close signal — not until the P&L ticker feels good. Replay-drill this 10 times/week; holding is a trainable reflex, not a personality trait.
- Interleave: alternate replay days between trend days and chop days so you learn which days NOT to add.
- **Stage gate:** eval passed (target $159K) or ≥ $6K cushion with consistency ≤ 50%. Expected around day 35–50 at your clean-day rate.

## Stage 3 — Days 51–90: "Funded Is a Different Sport"
**Goal: first payout. The hot streak that passes evals is statistically followed by a loss cluster — plan for it.**
- First 10 funded days: HALF size (3 micros), same process. Non-negotiable — this is where funded accounts die.
- Payout rhythm: bank the first payout at the minimum threshold, even if small. A banked $500 rewires more belief than a floating $3K.
- Losing-streak protocol (pre-written now, while calm): 2 red days in a row → next day 2 micros, 1 shot. 3 red days → 1 rest day + replay-only. Streaks are probability; the protocol converts fear into procedure.
- Keep the data sacred: any trade violating the plan gets mistake-tagged and EXCLUDED when judging the edge. Your edge is judged only on system trades.

## The rewritten operating system (your daily protocol, total ~25 min around a 1-2 hr session)
1. **Sleep ≥ 7h** or the app halves your limits — that rule already exists; respect why.
2. Pre-session (10 min): rules from memory → checklist → GO/NO-GO. NO-GO means no.
3. Session (19:00–20:00): 2-3 shots, one direction, flat size, loser cut at cap, winner held to level.
4. Post-session (10 min): CSV upload → read the grade → Feynman sentence per trade → tag playbooks.
5. 21:30: scheduled reconcile reports Lucid vs app + streak. Read it, close the screens.
6. Social: X only, and only after step 5. Instagram/Snapchat/Facebook deleted from the phone, not "avoided" — friction beats willpower.
7. App development: before 16:00 IST only, and from Stage 1 onward the app is FROZEN except bug fixes — building features is your dopamine leak, and the loop only works on a stable instrument.
