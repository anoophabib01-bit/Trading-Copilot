Live Mistake Log — Scalping (from Screen Recording Review)
Every mistake you identify goes here. This is your permanent evidence file — not rules, but your own patterns caught on tape.
M1
Entering on 1M — banned
Screen review confirmed: 1M entries = impulse, not edge. 1M view distorts structure, magnifies noise, and creates false confidence in bad setups.
MISTAKE
M2
Tunnel vision: watching only 1M warps your judgment
When you're zoomed into 1M for too long, every candle looks like a setup. You need the 15M context first — always.
MISTAKE
M3
TF drill-down order: 15M → 5M or 3M for entry. Never reverse.
15M gives direction. 5M/3M gives the entry candle. Skipping 15M means you're guessing the direction from the noise layer.
PROCESS
M4
After entry: hold for at least 2–3 closed candles on 3M before deciding
Exiting after 1 candle because it moved against you is not a decision — it's panic. Give the trade room to breathe: 2–3 closed 3M candles minimum.
EXIT
M5
Hold vs Exit rule: 3 consecutive candles OR 1 clear close in your direction = decision point
You do NOT need to wait for the next close after every entry. Identify 3 candles OR one decisive close, then make a calm hold/exit call. Not before.
EXIT
M6
The Breakeven Trap: 3M + 1M entries → loss → increase size → breakeven → stop for the day
This loop wastes hours without real losses — but it IS a loss. Time is capital. Fix: 2 losses = done for the session. No exceptions.

Stage 1 — One Clean Rep
Days 1–21 (5-green-day challenge, extended) · 15 sessions where process grade ≥ B. P&L explicitly does not count.
  • Daily: active-recall rules test (60 sec) → checklist → max 2-3 shots, 19:00–20:00 IST, 6 micros flat, one direction → CSV upload → read score + focus → one-sentence Feynman note per trade (2 min, in the day's plan notes).
  • Replay drill (3×/week, NOT during session hours): pick a random past NY open in TradingView replay, trade your playbook with replay_trade, 2 shots max. This is where volume-of-reps lives — sim reps are free, live reps cost eval fees.
  • Weekly (Saturday, 30 min): Insights review — score trend, avgW:avgL ratio, red-day causes. Write ONE sentence: "next week I will fix ___." One.
Stage gate: 5-green-day streak achieved + zero direction-flip days in the final week. Pass this and the eval math ($600/day pace) is already handling itself.
Stage 2 — Size the Winner, Not the Ego
Days 22–50 · avgW:avgL ≥ 1.5x. This is the profitability switch.
  • Now the MAE/MFE loop matters most: after each session, compute it. Target capture% climbing week over week (35% → 50%+). Method: JadeCap's risk-free add — stop to break-even on entry #1, add only after confirmation, never past 6 total.
  • Hold drill: on every winner, hold until either the next level or a 4H/1H candle-close signal — not until the P&L ticker feels good. Replay-drill this 10 times/week; holding is a trainable reflex, not a personality trait.
  • Interleave: alternate replay days between trend days and chop days so you learn which days NOT to add.
Stage gate: eval passed (target $159K) or ≥ $6K cushion with consistency ≤ 50%. Expected around day 35–50 at your clean-day rate.
Stage 3 — Funded Is a Different Sport
Days 51–90 · first payout. The hot streak that passes evals is statistically followed by a loss cluster — plan for it.
  • First 10 funded days: HALF size (3 micros), same process. Non-negotiable — this is where funded accounts die.
  • Payout rhythm: bank the first payout at the minimum threshold, even if small. A banked $500 rewires more belief than a floating $3K.
  • Losing-streak protocol (pre-written now, while calm): 2 red days in a row → next day 2 micros, 1 shot. 3 red days → 1 rest day + replay-only. Streaks are probability; the protocol converts fear into procedure.
  • Keep the data sacred: any trade violating the plan gets mistake-tagged and EXCLUDED when judging the edge. Your edge is judged only on system trades.
